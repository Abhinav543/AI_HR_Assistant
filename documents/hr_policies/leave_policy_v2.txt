# Employee Leave Policy

Policy Name: Employee Leave Policy
Policy Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 01 November 2026
Document Type: HR Policy

## 1. Annual Leave

All eligible employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave can be used for personal activities, vacations, and other planned absences.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 5 calendar days before the intended leave start date.

Annual leave approval depends on available leave balance and team workload.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave can be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical reasons.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when taking sick leave.

## 3. Leave Approval

All leave requests must be submitted through the company's leave management system.

Managers are responsible for reviewing leave requests.

The HR department may review requests involving policy exceptions or insufficient information.

Final approval or rejection must be provided by an authorized manager or HR representative.

## 4. Weekends and Public Holidays

Saturdays, Sundays, and officially recognized company holidays are excluded when calculating annual leave working days.

## 5. Leave Balance

Employees can request leave only within their available leave balance, subject to applicable policy exceptions and manager or HR review.

Employees can check their available annual and sick leave balances through the leave management system.

## 7. Carry-Forward of Unused Annual Leave

**Sample Demo Policy**

1. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.
2. Carried-forward leave must be used within the first 3 months of the following calendar year.
3. Unused carried-forward leave expires after 31 March.
4. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
5. The HR department is responsible for maintaining and verifying carried-forward leave balances.

## 8. Compensatory Off (Comp-Off) Policy

**Sample Demo Policy**

1. Employees who work on an approved weekly holiday or public holiday may be eligible for compensatory off.
2. Comp-off must be approved by the employee's manager before it is credited.
3. One approved extra working day earns one comp-off day.
4. Comp-off must be used before its recorded expiry date.
5. The expiry date assigned to each comp-off record is maintained in the HR database.
6. Expired comp-off cannot be used.
7. Comp-off cannot be used more than once.
8. Employees can check their available, used, and expired comp-off balances through the HR system.

## 9. Public Holidays During Leave

**Sample Demo Policy**

1. Official company public holidays falling within an annual or sick leave period are excluded from the leave-day calculation.
2. Saturdays and Sundays are also excluded from working-day calculations.
3. Employees are not charged leave for an official company holiday.
4. The company holiday calendar maintained by HR is the reference for determining public holidays.
5. Employees should check the holiday calendar before submitting leave requests.

## 10. Required Documents and Approval Process

**Sample Demo Policy**

1. Annual leave requests must include the leave type, start date, end date, and reason.
2. Sick leave requests must include the leave type, dates, and reason.
3. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days, a medical certificate should be submitted to HR.
4. Annual leave requests are reviewed by the employee's reporting manager.
5. Sick leave requests are reviewed by the reporting manager, with HR review where required.
6. Exceptions to standard leave rules require HR authorization.
7. A leave request remains Pending until an authorized manager or HR representative records a decision.
8. The AI assistant can provide recommendations but cannot independently approve or reject requests.

## 11. Escalation for Delayed Leave Approvals

**Sample Demo Policy**

1. Managers are expected to review pending leave requests within 2 working days of submission.
2. If no decision is recorded within 2 working days, the request is flagged as overdue.
3. The employee may contact HR to request an update.
4. HR may follow up with the reporting manager and review the delayed request.
5. An overdue request does not automatically become approved or rejected.
6. The final decision must be recorded by an authorized manager or HR representative.
