# Employee Leave Policy

Policy Name: Employee Leave Policy
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Document Type: HR Policy

## 1. Annual Leave

All eligible employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave can be used for personal activities, vacations, and other planned absences.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 calendar days before the intended leave start date.

Annual leave approval depends on available leave balance and team workload.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave can be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical reasons.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when taking sick leave.

## 3. Leave Approval

All leave requests must be submitted through the company's leave management system.

Managers are responsible for reviewing leave requests.

The HR department may review requests involving policy exceptions or insufficient information.

Submitting a leave request does not mean that the request has been approved.

Final approval or rejection must be provided by an authorized manager or HR representative.

## 4. Weekends and Public Holidays

Saturdays, Sundays, and officially recognized company holidays are excluded when calculating annual leave working days.

## 5. Leave Balance

Employees can request leave only within their available leave balance, subject to applicable policy exceptions and manager or HR review.

Employees can check their available annual and sick leave balances through the leave management system.
